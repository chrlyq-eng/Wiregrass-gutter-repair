Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Overview

Wiregrass Gutter Pros (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit wiregrassgutterpros.com or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you request a quote or contact us, we collect the information you choose to give us:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • Details about your home, project, or request
  • The page you submitted the form from

Like most websites, our hosting provider may also automatically log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit. This is used to keep the site secure and working properly.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide only to run our business and serve you, including to:

  • Respond to your quote request or question
  • Schedule, perform, and follow up on gutter services
  • Send estimates, invoices, and service-related messages
  • Improve our website and services
  • Protect against spam, fraud, and misuse of the site

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share it with service providers that help us run our website and business (for example, our website host, which processes form submissions on our behalf), or when required by law.

Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies to deliver the site securely.

Data Retention and Security

We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to you, provide services, and meet our legal and record-keeping obligations. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we have about you, or ask us to stop contacting you. To make a request, call us at (208) 715-1414 or send a message through our contact form.

Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, call us at (208) 715-1414 or send a message through our contact form.
